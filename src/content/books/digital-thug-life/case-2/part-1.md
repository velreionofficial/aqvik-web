## Is part ki seekh

**🚩 Is part mein thag ki chaalein**
1. **Laalach:** "18,450 reward points aaj raat expire ho rahe hain." Muft paisa + deadline = sochne ka time khatam.
2. **Asli lagne wali jaankari:** card ke aakhri 4 ank, card ki limit, naam — ye leak hue "lead sheet" se aate hain. Ye jaankari thag ke paas hona usko bank nahi bana deta.
3. **Darr ka dugna khel:** pehle laalach, phir "galti se charge laga, cancel karne ke liye OTP do." Har OTP ke saath paisa jaata hai, wapas nahi aata.
4. **"Main aapke saath hoon":** narm awaaz, maafi, "senior se approval" — taaki aap usse dost samjho.

**✅ Bachaav**
- **OTP kisi ko mat batao. Kabhi nahi. Bank wale ko bhi nahi.** OTP aapka signature hai — wo paisa *dene* ke liye hota hai, lene ke liye nahi.
- **SMS poora padho.** OTP ke saath amount aur dukaan ka naam likha hota hai. "Reward", "refund" ya "cancellation" ke liye kabhi OTP nahi lagta.
- Bank se baat karni ho to **khud call karo** — card ke peeche wale number pe, ya bank app se. Aane wali call pe bharosa mat karo.

**🆘 Agar aapke saath ho jaye**
1. **Card ke peeche wale number pe turant call** — card block karwao, dispute daalo
2. **1930** pe call (National Cyber Crime Helpline)
3. **cybercrime.gov.in** pe shikayat
4. Call aur number **Sanchar Saathi (Chakshu)** pe report karo
5. Saboot sambhaalo — SMS, call log, number, time
6. Jitni jaldi report, utna kam nuksaan — report ke baad wale transaction ki zimmedaari bank ki hoti hai

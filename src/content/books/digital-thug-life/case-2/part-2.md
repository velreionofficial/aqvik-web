### Page 1 — Card ke peeche wala number

Raat 11:09.

*"Avadh National Bank mein aapka swagat hai. Credit card se jude fraud ki report ke liye, 1 dabaiye."*

Deepak ne 1 dabaya. Phir 3. Phir apna card number. Imran sofa pe baitha tha, jaise kisi ne usme se hawa nikaal di ho.

Ek insaan ki awaaz aayi. Thaki hui, lekin saaf.

"Avadh National Bank, fraud monitoring team, main Arjun Mehra bol raha hoon. Bataiye."

Deepak ne ek saans mein sab bataya — reward points, teen OTP, teen baar ₹98,000, GoldKart Online.

Arjun ne beech mein ek bhi sawaal nahi kiya. Typing ki awaaz aati rahi.

"Theek hai. Sabse pehle — **card abhi block kar raha hoon.** Ho gaya. 11:14 PM. Iske baad is card pe jo bhi hoga, uski zimmedaari bank ki. Teeno transactions pe dispute daal diya hai. Complaint number SMS pe aayega."

"Paisa wapas aayega?" Imran ne pehli baar kuch bola.

Arjun ek second ruka. "Main aapse sach bolunga, Imran ji, kyunki thag aapse jhooth bol chuke hain. Abhi do kaam aur kijiye. Ek — **1930 pe call kijiye**, abhi, isi waqt. Do — kal subah **cybercrime.gov.in** pe shikayat. Baaki baat jaanch ke baad."

"National Cyber Crime Helpline. Aapki shikayat?"

Deepak ne phir se sab bataya. Is baar operator ne ek sawaal poochha jo kisi ne nahi poocha tha:

"Paisa kahan gaya? Merchant ka naam?"

"GoldKart Online. Teen baar. Gold coin."

"Achha." Keyboard ki awaaz. "Online gold wale merchant pe order turant deliver nahi hota. Hum merchant aur uske payment partner ko abhi alert bhej rahe hain. Agar order abhi dispatch nahi hua, to paisa ruk sakta hai. Aapka acknowledgement number SMS pe aa raha hai."

Raat 11:31. Teen transactions ko hue paanch ghante ho chuke the.

Imran ne Deepak ki taraf dekha. "Ruk sakta hai?"

"Ruk *sakta* hai," Deepak ne kaha. "Bhai, aaj raat tujhe ek hi baat seekhni hai — jitni jaldi report, utni zyada ummeed. Paanch ghante mein bhi kuch bach sakta hai. Paanch din mein nahi."

### Page 3 — Kalidih, agli subah

Pappu bhaiya ka phone baja. Doosre shehar se ek "delivery receiver" ka.

"Bhaiya, do packet mil gaye. Teesra nahi aaya. Courier wala bol raha hai *order on hold — payment under review.*"

Pappu bhaiya ne gaali di. Phir shaant hua. "Koi nahi. Do to mile. Teesre wale ka chakkar chhod de, aur us pate pe dobara mat jaana."

Golu ne suna. *Order on hold.* Matlab kal raat wala ladka — Imran — usne report kar diya tha. Jaldi.

Golu ko pata nahi kyun, achha laga.

Phir use darr laga. Agar uska paisa ruka, to kya *wo* bhi ruk sakte hain?

Pappu bhaiya ne uski aankhon mein dekha jaise man padh liya ho. "Darta kyun hai? SIM kisi aur ke naam ka, account kisi aur ke naam ka, delivery kisi aur ke pate pe. Hum kahin nahi hain, Golu. Hum hawa hain."

Hawa ka bhi pata chalta hai. Bas thoda waqt lagta hai.

### Page 4 — Bank ka khat

**Das din baad.**

Imran ke email pe bank ka jawab aaya. Deepak ne zor se padha:

> *"Dear Customer, your dispute regarding 3 transactions of Rs 98,000 each has been reviewed. Transaction 3 (Rs 98,000) has been reversed by the merchant as the order was not dispatched. For transactions 1 and 2, our investigation found that the transactions were authenticated with OTPs sent to your registered mobile, and that the OTPs were shared by you with a third party over a phone call. As per the bank's customer protection policy, losses arising from the customer sharing payment credentials are borne by the customer until the fraud is reported to the bank. The outstanding amount of Rs 1,96,000 remains payable."*

Kamre mein sannata tha.

"Ek… ek wapas aaya," Imran ne kaha. "₹98,000. Wo 1930 wala."

"Haan. Aur do nahi." Deepak ne laptop band kiya.

Imran ki aawaz phat gayi. "Lekin galti meri kaise? Unhone mujhe *dhokha* diya! Bank ka naam liya! Mera card number jaante the!"

### Page 5 — Deepak ka hisaab

Deepak ne ek kaagaz liya. CA ka student tha — hisaab kaagaz pe samajhta tha.

"Sun. Bank ka niyam, RBI ka niyam — main jitna samajhta hoon, seedha bata raha hoon:"

> **Agar galti bank ki ho, ya system mein kahin aur ho** — jaise data leak, hacking — aur tu teen working days ke andar report kar de, to **teri zimmedaari zero.**
>
> **Lekin agar tune khud OTP, PIN ya password kisi ko bataya** — to bank ise *customer ki laaparwahi* maanta hai. **Report karne tak ka saara nuksaan tera.** Report ke *baad* jo hua, wo bank ka.

"Isliye tera card 11:14 pe block hua — uske baad koi transaction hota, to bank bharta. Lekin teeno transaction usse pehle hue the, aur teeno tere OTP se."

Imran ne kaagaz ko ghoora. "Ye to… ye to bahut zaalim niyam hai."

"Shayad." Deepak ne kandhe uchkaye. "Lekin seedha hai. **OTP tera signature hai, Imran.** Jaise cheque pe sign karke kisi ko de do, to bank nahi kahega ki tumhare saath dhokha hua. Bank kahega — sign to aapka hai."

*(Note: RBI ne naye niyam ghoshit kiye hain jo 1 January 2027 se laagu honge — usme chhote fraud pe kuch bharpaai ka intezaam hai. Dekho kahani ke ant mein "Ye asli hai".)*

### Page 6 — Ammi

Us raat Imran ne Ammi ko video call kiya. Pehle kuch der chup raha. Phir sab bata diya.

Ammi ne beech mein nahi toka. Jab Imran ruka, to bas itna kaha:

"Kitna gaya?"

"Ek lakh chhiyanve hazaar, Ammi. Card pe. Bharna padega."

Lambi chuppi. Phir Ammi ne woh kaha jo sirf ammiyan kehti hain:

"Paisa gaya hai beta. Tum to nahi gaye. Paisa kama lenge."

Imran ro pada. Teen mahine pehle usne card dikha ke kaha tha *ab aapka beta bhi card wala hai.*

"Aur sun," Ammi ne kaha. "Bank walon se baat karke ise kishton mein karwa. Aur aaj se jo bhi call aaye, pehle mujhse poochhna. Main 54 saal ki hoon, mujhe OTP ka matlab nahi pata — lekin mujhe itna pata hai ki jo anjaan aadmi paisa dene ki baat kare, wo paisa lene aaya hai."

Agle din Imran ne baaki raqam **12 kishton** (EMI) mein badalwa li. Har mahine lagbhag **₹18,000.** Tankhwah ₹32,000.

**Do mahine baad.**

### Page 11 — Case 2 ka saar

**🚩 OTP scam ko kaise pehchaanein — 5 nishaan**
1. **Aane wali call** jo "bank", "rewards", "card upgrade" ya "KYC" ki baat kare
2. **Laalach ya darr ki deadline** — "aaj raat expire", "abhi cancel nahi hua to byaj"
3. **OTP maangna** — kisi bhi bahaane: verification, cancellation, refund, reversal
4. **"Amount pe dhyaan mat dijiye"** — SMS mein likhi raqam ko jhutlana
5. **Ek ke baad ek OTP** — har baar naya bahaana

**✅ Ek line yaad rakho: OTP = aapka signature**
- OTP sirf tab, jab **aap khud** kuch kharid rahe ho, aur SMS mein likhi raqam aur dukaan **aapki** ho.
- Reward, refund, cancellation, KYC — **kisi ke liye OTP nahi lagta.**
- Bank se baat: **khud call karo**, card ke peeche wale number pe ya bank app se.

**🆘 Agar aapke saath ho jaye**
1. **Card ke peeche wale number pe call** — card block, dispute
2. **1930** — turant (online kharidari wala paisa kai baar ruk jaata hai)
3. **cybercrime.gov.in** — shikayat, acknowledgement number sambhaalo
4. Number **Sanchar Saathi (Chakshu)** pe report karo
5. **Saboot** — SMS, call log, time; kuch delete mat karo
6. Bacha hua bill **EMI** mein badalwane ki baat bank se karo

**💬 Ise share karo:** apne naye-naye card wale dosto aur ghar ke group pe. Ek OTP, ek signature.

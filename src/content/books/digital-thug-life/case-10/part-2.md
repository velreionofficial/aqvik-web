### Page 1 — 41 parivaar

**Do din baad. Cyber Police Station, Nagpur.**

Inspector Swati Bhagat ne Aditya ke saamne 41 kaagaz bichhaaye. Ek-ek kar ke.

"Padho."

Aditya ne pehla uthaya. *Pooja Deshmukh, Nagpur. ₹1,96,000. "Tax clearance." Shaadi ke paise.*

Doosra. *Ravi Kumar Mahto, Ranchi. ₹9,800. Loan app "late fee". Delivery rider.*

Teesra. *Ek 71 saal ke retired teacher, Lucknow. ₹2,40,000. Pension.*

Chautha. Paanchva. Chhatha.

Har kaagaz pe ek naam. Ek shehar. Ek kahani. Aur har kaagaz ke neeche ek hi line: *Labhaarthi khaata: A. Verma.*

Aditya ke haath kaanpne lage. Teen mahine se wo sochta raha tha *"main kisi ko loot nahi raha."* Aaj 41 log uske saamne the. Kaagaz pe. Lekin asli.

"Madam… mujhe nahi pata tha…"

"Tumhe pata nahi tha kaun," Inspector Bhagat ne kaha. "Lekin tum jaante the ki kuch galat hai. Warna Rocky ne kyun kaha *'kisi ko batana mat'*? Aur tumne kyun nahi bataya?"

Aditya ke paas koi jawaab nahi tha.

### Page 2 — Kanoon

"Main thag nahi hoon, madam," Aditya ne aakhir kaha. "Maine kisi ko phone nahi kiya. Kisi se OTP nahi maanga."

Inspector Bhagat kursi pe peeche hui.

"Ek baat samjho, Aditya. Thag ke paas phone hai, script hai, nakli app hai. Lekin ek cheez nahi hai — *ek khaata jo uske naam pe na ho.* Kyunki apne naam pe khaata khola to pakda jaayega. Isliye use tum chahiye. Tumhara naam. Tumhara chehra. Tumhara KYC."

Unhone 41 kaagaz ki taraf ishaara kiya.

"Tumhare khaate ke bina, in 41 mein se zyadatar ka paisa jaa hi nahi paata. Tum thag nahi ho. Lekin tum wo darwaaza ho jisse thag andar aaya. Aise maamlon mein police khaata-dhaarak pe bhi dhokhadhadi ki dhaaraayein lagaati hai, aur gang ho to sangathit apraadh ki bhi. Faisla court karega — lekin case tumhare naam pe chalega."

Aditya ne pehli baar ek shabd suna jo use zindagi bhar yaad rahega: **mule.**

"Jaanwar ki tarah," Inspector ne kaha. "Bojh dhone wala. Bojh kiska hai, usse matlab nahi. Bas dhona hai. Aur jab pakda jaaye — maar bhi usi ko padti hai."

### Page 3 — 23 din

Aditya **23 din** jail mein raha.

Ek baraak. 40 log. Usme se chaar uske jaise the — 19, 21, 22 saal ke. Kisi ne ₹3,000 mein khaata diya tha. Kisi ne ₹10,000 mein. Ek ne apni maa ke naam pe bhi khulwaaya tha.

Varanasi mein papa ne chai ki dukaan teen din band rakhi. Phir ghat ke chai walon ki samiti se karz liya. Vakeel ki fees. Nagpur ka kiraya.

Zamaanat ke din papa court ke bahar khade the. Ek purane kurte mein. Aditya bahar aaya to papa ne gale nahi lagaya. Bas uska haath pakda. Kas ke.

Train mein, raat ko, papa ne pehli baar kuch kaha:

"30 saal se chai bechta hoon, Aditya. Ek kulhad bhi kisi ka chura ke nahi becha. Ek bhi." Unhone khidki se bahar dekha. "Aur mere bete ke naam pe ₹63 lakh ki chori."

Aditya ne sar unke kandhe pe rakha. Bachpan ke baad pehli baar.

"Papa, main theek karunga. Jitna ho sake."

### Page 4 — Gawaah

Aditya ne wo kiya jo Rocky ne kabhi socha nahi tha. Usne sab bataya.

Canteen ka wo kona. Lifaafa dene ka din. Rocky ka number. ChatBox ki purani chat ke screenshot — jo usne kabhi delete nahi kiye the. Monu ka naam bhi — aur Monu ko khud saath le gaya.

"Monu, chal. Khud chal ke bata. Darwaaza khatkhatane se pehle."

Monu darr raha tha. Lekin gaya. Apna khaata band karwaaya. Bayaan diya. Inspector Bhagat ne dono ki baat likhi. "Khud aakar batana, khaata band karwaana, poora sahyog — ye sab case mein maayne rakhta hai," unhone kaha. "Lekin kanoon apna kaam karega. Main koi vaada nahi kar sakti."

Teen hafte baad **Rocky** pakda gaya. Lucknow ke ek hotel se. Uske phone mein **86 "kit"** ki list thi — 86 college ke ladke-ladkiyaan, chaar shehron se.

Rocky ke phone se ek aur number mila. *"Controller."*

Wo abhi tak nahi pakda gaya. Lekin uski list mein ab 86 khaate kam the.

### Page 5 — Court

**Do mahine baad. Nagpur. Ek court room.**

Aaj ek arzi ki sunwaai thi: *Pooja Deshmukh vs. — freeze kiye gaye ₹1,08,000 ki vaapsi.* Wo khaata — Aditya ka tha.

Aditya ko gawaah ke taur pe bulaaya gaya tha.

Court ke bahar ek bench pe ek ladki baithi thi. Saath mein ek bujurg aadmi, safed kurta. Aditya ne naam suna to pehchaan gaya. Kaagaz number ek.

Pooja Deshmukh.

Wo uthi. Aditya ki taraf aayi. Aditya ke pair zameen mein gad gaye.

"Tum Aditya ho?" Pooja ki awaaz shaant thi.

"Ji…" Aditya ne nazrein neeche kar leen. "Didi, mujhe maaf—"

"Wo ₹1,96,000," Pooja ne kaha, "mere papa ke the. 34 saal bus ki ticket kaat ke jode the. Meri shaadi ke liye. Ek raat mein, tumhare khaate se, das minute mein, kahin aur chale gaye."

Aditya ki aankhon se aansu gir rahe the. Court ke galiyare mein. Sab ke saamne.

Pooja ke papa — **Sudhakar ji** — aage aaye. Aditya ko dekha. Der tak.

"Tumne kitne liye the, beta?"

"₹15,000, uncle."

Sudhakar ji ne gehri saans li. "Galti maan li — aadha sudhar gaya. Baaki aadha?" Unhone Aditya ke kandhe pe haath rakha. "Jaake apne jaise das ladkon ko bata. Phir pura hoga."

Andar judge ne aadesh padha: *₹1,08,000 shikayatkarta ko lautaaye jaayein.*

### Page 6 — Keemat

₹15,000 ki asli keemat dheere-dheere saamne aayi.

**Bank:** Aditya ke naam ke saare khaate band. Naya khaata kholne gaya to — *"Sorry, aapka KYC flagged hai."*

**College:** ek semester ka suspension. Scholarship ruki.

**Case:** chalta rahega. Shayad saalon tak. Har peshi pe Nagpur ka safar.

**Naukri:** pehle interview mein HR ne poochha — *"Koi criminal case pending?"* Aditya ne sach bataya. Phone kabhi wapas nahi aaya.

**Ghar:** papa ka karz. ₹70,000. Har mahine ₹4,000 — chai ki dukaan se.

Khushi ne ek din apni diary mein likha, jo Aditya ne galti se padh li:

*"Bhaiya ke naye joote ab bhi almari mein hain. Wo pehente nahi. Kehte hain unme ₹63 lakh ki boo aati hai."*

### Page 7 — Ghat ki dukaan

Papa ki chai ki dukaan pe ab ek naya board tha. Aditya ne khud likha tha, haath se:

*"☕ CHAI ₹10*
*⚠ KHAATA KIRAYE PE MAT DO. ₹5,000 ke badle JAIL milti hai. Mujhse poochho — maine diya tha. — Aditya*

Pehle din log hanse. Kuch ne photo kheenchi. Ek bujurg ne kaha, "Ye kya likh diya, Shankar? Grahak bhaag jaayenge."

Lekin teesre din, do ladke aaye. College ke. Chai pi. Phir ek ne dhire se poochha:

"Bhaiya… ek senior ne kaha hai ₹6,000 mahina… khaata ke liye. Aap… aapke saath kya hua tha?"

Aditya ne unhe baithaaya. Ek ghanta baat ki. 41 kaagaz ki. 23 din ki. Court ke galiyare ki.

Jaate waqt ek ladke ne kaha, "Bhaiya, main kal hi us senior ko mana kar dunga. Aur 1930 pe bataunga."

Agle mahine aise **17 ladke** aaye. Kuch ne mana kiya. Kuch ne khud jaake apne khaate band karwaaye.

Sudhakar ji ne kaha tha das. Aditya 17 tak pahunch gaya tha.

### Page 10 — Case 10 ka saar

**🚩 "Khaata kiraye pe" ka jaal kaise pehchaanein — 5 nishaan**
1. **"Bina kuch kiye ₹3,000–₹10,000 mahina"** — senior, dost, social media ad
2. **Khaata + card + SIM + net banking password** — "sab ek lifaafe mein"
3. **"Gaming company", "tax bachaana", "crypto trading"** — jhoothi kahani
4. **"Kisi ko batana mat"**
5. **Referral bonus** — "dost laao, ₹1,000 lo"

**✅ Yaad rakho**
- **Aapka khaata = aapki zimmedaari.** Usme jo bhi paisa aaye-jaaye, jawaab aapko dena hoga.
- Mule account dene wala bhi **aaropi** ban sakta hai — dhokhadhadi, sangathit apraadh.
- Jaanch ke dauraan **aapke doosre khaaton pe bhi rok** lag sakti hai. KYC, naukri, padhai — sab pe asar pad sakta hai.
- ₹5,000 ke badle: **jail, case, karz, aur 41 parivaaron ke aansu.**

**🆘 Agar aapne khaata de diya hai**
1. **Aaj hi bank jaao** — khaata band karwao, card/net banking/UPI block
2. Diya hua **SIM band karwao**
3. **1930** aur **cybercrime.gov.in** pe khud batao
4. **Cyber police station** jaao — khud aakar sach batana, darwaaze pe police aane se behtar hai
5. Jisne liya, uska naam, number, chat — sab sambhaal ke rakho

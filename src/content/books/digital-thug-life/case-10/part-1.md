### Page 1 — Cold open

**Varanasi. Assi ke paas ek gali. Mangalvaar, subah 5:10.**

Darwaaze pe teen zor ki thaap.

Shankar Verma baniyan mein the. Haath mein chai ka bhagona — ghat pe dukaan kholne ki taiyaari. Darwaaza khola to saamne chaar log. Do vardi mein, do saadi.

"Aditya Verma yahin rehta hai?"

"Ji… mera beta hai. Kya hua?"

"Nagpur Cyber Police. Aur ye local thane se." Ek mahila officer ne kaagaz dikhaya. "Aapke bete ke naam ke do bank khaate hain. Unke khilaaf **41 shikayatein** hain. **Nau rajyon** se."

Bhagona Shankar ji ke haath se gira. Garam chai farsh pe phail gayi.

Andar ke kamre mein 20 saal ka Aditya ankh malte hue utha. Darwaaze pe police dekhi. Aur uske pet mein wo thanda gola utha jo teen mahine se kahin dabaa pada tha.

Usne kisi ko thaga nahi tha. Kisi ko phone nahi kiya tha. Kisi se OTP nahi maanga tha.

Usne bas apna khaata kiraye pe diya tha. **₹5,000 mahina.**

### Page 2 — ₹600 roz

Aditya Verma, B.A. doosra saal. Ghar mein papa, mummy **Geeta**, aur chhoti behen **Khushi** — 14 saal, nauvi mein, padhai mein tez.

Papa ki ghat pe chai ki chhoti si dukaan thi. 30 saal se. Achhe din mein ₹600 bachte the. Baarish wale din kuch nahi.

College mein Aditya ke dost naye phone pe reels banaate the. Kisi ke paas bike thi, kisi ke paas branded joote. Aditya ke paas teen saal purana phone tha, jiski screen pe ek laambi daraar thi.

Ek din canteen mein ek senior ne uski daraar dekhi. Hansa.

"Bhai, ye phone? 2026 mein?"

Senior ka naam tha **Rocky bhaiya**. 24 saal. Final year mein teen saal se. Lekin sabse mehenga phone, sabse nayi bike, aur hamesha jeb mein note.

"Kaise, bhaiya?" Aditya ne poochha. "Aapke papa kya karte hain?"

Rocky muskuraya. "Papa nahi, bhai. *Main* karta hoon. Bata, ₹5,000 mahina chahiye? Bina kuch kiye?"

### Page 3 — "Bas khaata"

"Ek gaming company hai," Rocky ne dhire se samjhaaya. "Bahut paisa aata-jaata hai. Tax bachaane ke liye wo alag-alag logon ke khaate use karte hain. Tu bas ek naya khaata khol. Ek naya SIM le. Card, passbook, net banking ka password, aur wo SIM — sab ek lifaafe mein mujhe de de."

"Aur?"

"Aur kuch nahi. Har mahine ₹5,000 cash. Tere haath mein. Khaata tera, kaam unka."

Aditya ruka. "Bhaiya, ye… legal hai?"

Rocky hansa. "Bhai, aadha college karta hai. Kabhi kisi ko kuch hua? Tera khaata hai, tera paisa nahi. Tu thodi na kisi ko loot raha hai."

*Tu thodi na kisi ko loot raha hai.* Ye line Aditya ko teen mahine tak sulaati rahi.

Agle hafte Aditya ne do alag bankon mein khaate khole. Ek naya SIM liya — apne naam pe. Sab ek bhure lifaafe mein rakha. Canteen ke peeche Rocky ko diya.

Rocky ne jeb se ₹5,000 nikaale. Kadak note.

### Page 5 — "KASHI-07"

**Usi raat. Kisi doosre shehar ka ek kamra. Deewar pe ek bada screen.**

Screen pe ek list thi. Sau se zyada naam. Har ek ke saath ek code.

*KASHI-07 — A. Verma — 2 khaate — Status: ACTIVE ✅*

Ek aadmi — jise sab bas **"Controller"** kehte the — ek-ek khaate ko alag-alag "kaam" pe lagata tha.

"KASHI-07 ka pehla khaata — Nagpur wale task group ko de do. Doosra — loan recovery wale ko."

Agle teen mahine KASHI-07 ke khaaton mein paisa aaya. Aur gaya. Har baar das minute ke andar.

> *Nagpur se ₹1,96,000* — "tax clearance", kisi ladki ki shaadi ke paise.
> *Ranchi se ₹9,800* — kisi delivery rider ki "late fee".
> *Lucknow se ₹2,40,000.* *Surat se ₹88,000.* *Kochi se ₹3,10,000.*

Har credit ke peeche ek parivaar. Har parivaar mein koi ro raha tha. Koi chhupa raha tha. Koi 1930 dabaa raha tha.

Aur Aditya, Varanasi mein, naye jooton mein, ghat pe dost ke saath selfie le raha tha.

Kul — teen mahine mein — **₹63 lakh** uske naam ke khaaton se guzre.

Aditya ke haath aaye: **₹15,000.**

### Page 6 — Monu

Doosre mahine Aditya ne apne bachpan ke dost **Monu** ko Rocky se milwaaya.

"Kuch nahi hota, yaar. Teen mahine se le raha hoon. Dekh, naye joote."

Monu ke papa rickshaw chalaate the. Monu ko bhi ₹5,000 chahiye the. Usne bhi khaata khola. Lifaafa diya.

Aditya ko ₹1,000 mile. "Referral."

Us raat Aditya ko neend nahi aayi. Pata nahi kyun. Ek ajeeb sa bhaar.

Usne khud ko samjhaaya: *Main kisi ko loot nahi raha. Main bas khaata de raha hoon. Paisa unka, kaam unka.*

Lekin ek sawaal dimaag mein ghoomta raha, jo Rocky ne kabhi nahi bataya tha:

*Ek "gaming company" ko 20 saal ke ladkon ke khaate kyun chahiye? Unke apne khaate kya hue?*

Usne us sawaal ko takiye ke neeche daba diya. Agli 5 tareekh ko phir ₹5,000 aane the.

### Page 7 — Freeze

Teesre mahine, Aditya college ki fees bharne bank gaya. Apne **purane** khaate se — jisme uski scholarship ke ₹18,000 the. Rocky wale khaaton se alag.

Cashier ne screen dekhi. Phir Aditya ko. Phir manager ko bulaaya.

"Aditya ji, aapke is khaate pe **debit freeze** hai. Police ki request. Aapke naam ke doosre khaaton mein cyber fraud ki shikayatein hain."

"Lekin ye khaata alag hai! Isme meri scholarship—"

"Sir, freeze aapke naam pe hai. Hum kuch nahi kar sakte. Police se baat kariye."

Aditya bahar aaya. Kaanpte haath se Rocky ko call kiya.

*"The number you have dialled does not exist."*

ChatBox pe dekha. Rocky ki profile photo gayab. *"Last seen: long time ago."*

Wo ghat ki seedhiyon pe baith gaya. Papa ki dukaan door se dikh rahi thi. Papa ek grahak ko kulhad mein chai de rahe the. Muskuraate hue.

## Is part ki seekh

**🚩 Is part mein thag ki chaalein**
1. **"Bina kuch kiye ₹5,000 mahina":** aisa koi kaam nahi hota.
2. **"Gaming company", "tax bachaana":** jhoothi kahani taaki aap sawaal na poochho.
3. **Khaata + card + SIM + password ek saath:** iska matlab — aapka khaata ab thag ka hai.
4. **"Aadha college karta hai, kisi ko kuch nahi hua":** hota hai. Bas aapko pata nahi chalta, jab tak darwaaze pe police na aaye.
5. **Referral ka laalach:** dost ko phansaao, ₹1,000 kamaao.

**✅ Bachaav**
- **Apna khaata, card, SIM, net banking password, UPI — kabhi kisi ko mat do.** Kisi bhi keemat pe.
- "Khaata kiraye pe" = **mule account.** Thag ke saath aap bhi aaropi ban sakte ho.
- Ek freeze **aapke naam ke doosre khaaton** tak pahunch sakta hai — scholarship, salary, sab.
- Koi senior, dost ya "bhaiya" aisa offer de — **mana karo, aur 1930 pe batao.**

**🆘 Agar aapne khaata de diya hai**
1. **Turant bank jaao** — khaata band/freeze karwao, card aur net banking block karo
2. **SIM band karwao** jo aapne diya tha
3. **1930** aur **cybercrime.gov.in** pe khud batao — kisne liya, kab, kaise
4. Najdeeki **cyber police station** jaao — khud aakar sach batana, darr ke chhupne se behtar hai
5. Jisne aapko phansaaya, uska naam, number, chat — sab sambhaal ke rakho

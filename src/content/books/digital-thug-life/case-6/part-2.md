### Page 1 — Subah 6:20

Pooja raat bhar nahi soyi.

Subah 6:20 baje chhota bhai **Sahil** — 19 saal, polytechnic mein — paani peene utha to didi ko hall mein baitha dekha. Aankhein soojhi hui. Haath mein diary.

"Didi? Tu soyi nahi?"

Pooja ne kuch kehna chaha. Awaaz nahi nikli. Bas laptop uski taraf ghuma diya. Dashboard. Chat. Payment ke screenshot. Ek ke baad ek.

Sahil padhta gaya. Uska chehra badalta gaya.

"Didi… ye papa ke…"

"Haan."

Sahil ek pal chup raha. Phir wo kiya jo Pooja ne 11 din mein ek baar bhi nahi kiya tha — usne kisi aur ko bataya.

"Phone de. **1930.** Hamare college mein cyber wale aaye the. Bole the — jitni jaldi, utna achha."

Subah saat baje papa nahaa-dho ke taiyaar the. Safed kurta. Jeweller ke yahan jaane ke liye.

"Chal Pooja, der ho rahi hai. Seth ji ne saade aath ka time diya hai."

Pooja khadi hui. Phir baith gayi. Phir papa ke pairon ke paas baith gayi.

"Papa… jeweller ke yahan nahi jaana."

Aur phir sab nikal aaya. ₹150. Riya ma'am. Group. Combination. Freeze. Tax. **₹3,92,000.**

Mummy **Vandana** ka haath muh pe tha. Sahil deewar se laga khada tha.

Papa ne kuch nahi kaha. Uthe. Bahar balcony mein gaye. Paanch minute. Das minute.

Pooja ko laga ab chilla-chilla ke daantenge. Ya ro denge. Ya — sabse bura — kuch nahi kahenge, kabhi nahi.

Papa wapas aaye. Pooja ke saamne baithe. Aur dhire se bole:

"34 saal bus mein ticket kaati hai, Pooja. Har roz koi na koi bina ticket chadhta tha, jhooth bolta tha. Chor ko chor kehna seekha hai maine." Unhone beti ka sar uthaya. "Tune galti ki. Ye sach hai. Lekin chor tu nahi hai. Chor wo hai."

### Page 10 — Case 6 ka saar

**🚩 Task / "like karo, kamao" scam ko kaise pehchaanein — 5 nishaan**
1. **Anjaan number se job offer** — "Hi dear", "part-time", "₹5,000 roz"
2. **Pehle chhote paise** — ₹150, ₹1,300 — bharosa jeetne ke liye
3. **Group mein sab kama rahe hain** — screenshot, "thank you ma'am", 🎉
4. **"Prepaid / merchant task"** — kaam ke liye *aap* paisa bharo
5. **Freeze, combination, credit score, tax** — paisa nikaalne ke liye aur paisa

**✅ Ek line yaad rakho: jo naukri pehle paisa maange, wo naukri nahi**
- Asli naukri: interview → offer letter → salary. Ulta kabhi nahi.
- "Withdraw ke liye tax/fee" = fraud. Asli TDS kamaai se kat-ta hai, alag se nahi maanga jaata.
- Job sirf **jaane-maane job portal ya company ki official website** se. ChatBox pe aaye "HR" se nahi.
- Ghar mein **kisi ek ko pehle din bata do.** Chuppi thag ki sabse badi taakat hai.
- Ho gaya ho, to **sharam mat karo.** Asha tai ki tarah saal bhar chhupaana, thag ko agla shikaar deta hai.

**🆘 Agar aapke saath ho jaye**
1. **1930** — turant. Har UTR number taiyaar rakho
2. **cybercrime.gov.in** — chat, group, payment ke screenshot ke saath
3. **Bank** ko bhi batao
4. Number **Sanchar Saathi (Chakshu)** pe report karo
5. Freeze hua paisa **court ke order** se wapas milta hai — police ke saath arzi lagao
6. "Paisa wapas dilaane" wale kisi call pe bharosa nahi — wo doosra thag hai

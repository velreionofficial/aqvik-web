## Is part ki seekh

**🚩 Is part mein thag ki chaalein**
1. **Anjaan number se "job offer":** "Like karo, review do, ₹50 kamao" — ye naukri nahi hai.
2. **Pehle chhote paise dena:** ₹150, ₹1,300 — taaki aapko lage ye sach hai. Ye daana hai.
3. **Nakli group:** 46 member, 41 nakli. Khush logon ke screenshot banaye jaate hain.
4. **"Prepaid task":** kaam karne ke liye pehle *aap* paise do — duniya ki koi asli naukri aisa nahi karti.
5. **Freeze, combination, tax:** har baar ek naya bahana — taaki pichhla paisa bachaane ke liye aap aur daalo.

**✅ Bachaav**
- **Jo naukri pehle paisa maange, wo naukri nahi.** Bas itna yaad rakho.
- Asli company interview leti hai, offer letter deti hai, salary deti hai — "task" ke badle "recharge" nahi maangti.
- "Withdraw karne ke liye tax/fee bharo" = 100% fraud. Asli TDS aapki kamaai mein se kat-ta hai, alag se nahi maanga jaata.
- Ghar ke kisi ek insaan ko **pehle din hi** bata do. Thag ki sabse badi taakat aapki chuppi hai.

**🆘 Agar aapke saath ho jaye**
1. **1930** pe turant call — har transaction ka UTR number taiyaar rakho
2. **cybercrime.gov.in** pe shikayat — chat, group aur payment ke screenshot ke saath
3. Bank ko call — jin khaaton mein paisa gaya, unki jaankaari do
4. Number **Sanchar Saathi (Chakshu)** pe report karo
5. **"Paisa wapas dilaane" wale** kisi agent pe bharosa mat karo — wo doosra thag hai

## Is part ki seekh

**🚩 Is part mein thag ki chaalein**
1. **Nakli awaaz:** public reels se bachche ki awaaz uthaakar software se "rota hua beta" banaaya gaya.
2. **Sahi waqt:** "phone off" wali story padh ke tab call kiya jab bachcha pahunch se baahar tha.
3. **"Kisi ko phone mat karo, line pe raho":** taaki aap sach check na kar pao.
4. **"Settlement", "izzat", "media":** dar aur sharm — dimaag band karne ke liye.
5. **Paisa kisi "Enterprises" ke khaate mein:** police ka nahi, vakeel ka nahi — thag ka.
6. **Ek ke baad ek maang:** settlement, phir hospital, phir aur.

**✅ Bachaav**
- **Phone kaato. Bachche ko uske apne number pe call karo.** Na lage to uske dost, hostel, roommate, office ko.
- **Parivaar ka "code word" banao** — ek sawaal jiska jawaab sirf apne log jaante hain. Tanvi ka "Bruno ka biscuit" yahi tha.
- **Police kabhi phone pe "settlement" nahi karti,** aur UPI pe paisa nahi maangti.
- **Video call maango.** "Camera toot gaya", "network nahi" — shak badhao, ghatao nahi.
- Bachche: **"Phone off", "ghar se door", "akela hoon"** — aisi baatein public story mein mat daalo.

**🆘 Agar aapke saath ho jaye**
1. **1930** pe turant call — har payment ka UTR, aur call wale number ke saath
2. **cybercrime.gov.in** pe shikayat — call log, UPI screenshot
3. **Bank** ko call — jin khaaton mein paisa gaya
4. Number **Sanchar Saathi (Chakshu)** pe report karo
5. Agle din bhi koi "case band karne" ke liye call kare — wo bhi thag hi hai

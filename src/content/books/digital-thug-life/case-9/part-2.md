"Papa? Aap ro kyun rahe ho? Mummy?"

Aryan ke dost peeche chup ho gaye the. Ek ne dhire se kaha, "Bhai… teri reels."

"Papa," usne fusfusaaya. "Maine khud unhe bataya ki kab call karna hai."

Speaker se ek awaaz aayi: *"Namaste, main SI Aditi Chouhan hoon, aur ye meri asli awaaz nahi hai."*

Awaaz bilkul unki thi.

"Ye maine apne ek purane interview ke 40 second se banaayi hai, sir. Training ke liye. Aaj kal ye software khule mein milte hain. 30–40 second ki saaf awaaz kaafi hai." Unhone laptop band kiya. "Aapke bete ki to 4 ghante ki awaaz public thi."

"Ek baat yaad rakhiye, ma'am," SI Chouhan ne kaha. "Police kabhi phone pe settlement nahi karti. Kabhi UPI pe paisa nahi maangti. Aur kabhi nahi kehti ki *'kisi ko phone mat karna'*. Ye teen baatein jo bhi kahe — wo police nahi hai."

### Page 10 — Case 9 ka saar

**🚩 Nakli awaaz / "beta musibat mein hai" scam ko kaise pehchaanein — 6 nishaan**
1. **Raat ko, anjaan number se**, rota hua "beta/beti"
2. **Accident, giraftaari, kidnapping** — badi musibat, abhi ke abhi
3. **"Kisi ko phone mat karna", "line pe raho"**
4. **Phone pe "settlement"** — police, vakeel, ya "ladki ke ghar wale"
5. **Paisa kisi "Enterprises" ya anjaan ke khaate mein**
6. **Video call nahi** — "camera toot gaya", "network nahi"

**✅ Teen kadam — har baar**
- **Phone kaato.** Bachche ko uske apne number pe, ya uske dost/hostel/office ko call karo.
- **Code word poocho.** Aaj hi parivaar ke saath ek banao — jo public mein kahin na ho.
- **Police phone pe paisa nahi maangti.** Kabhi nahi.

**✅ Bachchon ke liye**
- Public reels/stories mein **"phone off", "akela hoon", "ghar se door"** mat daalo
- Account **private** rakho, ya kam se kam ghar-parivaar ki jaankaari mat daalo
- Mummy-papa ko **dost/roommate ka number** de ke rakho

**🆘 Agar aapke saath ho jaye**
1. **1930** — turant, har UTR aur call wale number ke saath
2. **cybercrime.gov.in** — call log, payment ke screenshot
3. **Bank** ko call
4. Number **Sanchar Saathi (Chakshu)** pe report karo
5. **Sharm mat karo** — ye awaaz kisi ko bhi dhokha de sakti hai. Batao, taaki agla parivaar bache.

**💬 Ise share karo:** apne parivaar ke group mein. Aur aaj raat ek code word bana lo.

## Is part ki seekh

**🚩 Is part mein thag ki chaalein**
1. **Nakli adalat aur kaagaz:** gown, hathoda, "order", mohar wale PDF — sab asli dikhne ke liye. Asli court kabhi video call pe kisi ko "bail" ke liye paisa transfer karne ko nahi kehti.
2. **"Refundable" ka jhaansa:** "surety", "verification", "tax clearance" — har baar naya naam, aur vaada ki paisa wapas aayega. Kabhi nahi aata.
3. **Sunk cost trap:** jitna paisa ja chuka, utna hi aadmi aur deta hai taaki pehla wapas mile. Thag yahi chahte hain.
4. **Paisa alag-alag accounts mein:** "Shree Ganesh Traders", "Maa Durga Enterprises" — ye RBI ke nahi, kiraye pe liye gaye (mule) accounts hote hain.

**✅ Bachaav**
- **RBI kabhi kisi aam aadmi ka paisa "verify" karne ke liye apne account mein nahi mangwata.** Koi bhi "nodal/safe/verification account" = fraud.
- Bank mein staff poochhe "sab theek hai?" to **sach bolo.** Bank wale aapki madad ke liye hain, aur bade transfer pe ruk sakte hain.
- Ghar ke buzurg achanak kamra band rakhne lagein, bank ke chakkar lagayein, ya phone pe "sir… ji sir" kehte rahein — **unse pyaar se poochho.** Darr mein insaan khud nahi bolta.

**🆘 Agar aapke saath ho jaye**
1. **1930** pe turant call (jitni jaldi, utna zyada paisa rukne ka mauka)
2. **cybercrime.gov.in** pe shikayat
3. **Bank** ko turant batao — transfer aur account rukwao
4. Number **Sanchar Saathi (Chakshu)** pe report karo
5. Saboot sambhaalo — SMS, PDF, screenshots, account naam
6. Darr lage to **112**

## Is part ki seekh

**🚩 Is part mein thag ki chaalein**
1. **Darr:** "Parcel mein drugs", "FIR darj", "arrest warrant" — pehle 2 minute mein itna darr ki sochne ka time na mile.
2. **Akelapan:** "Kisi ko mat batana, wo bhi phansega." Ghar walon se kaat dena thag ka sabse bada hathiyaar hai.
3. **Asli lagne wali detail:** Aadhaar ke aakhri 4 ank, beti ki shaadi ki date, akhbaar ki photo — ye sab leak data aur aapke hi social media se aata hai.

**✅ Bachaav**
- **Kanoon mein "digital arrest" naam ki koi cheez nahi hoti.** Koi police, CBI, court ya RBI video call pe giraftaar nahi karti, aur kabhi "verification" ke liye paisa nahi maangti.
- Aisi call aaye to **call kaato, ghar walon ko turant batao.** Thag sabse zyada isi se darte hain.
- Apni aur parivaar ki private detail (shaadi ki date, ghar, bank) public reels/posts mein mat daalo.

**🆘 Agar aapke saath ho jaye**
1. **1930** pe turant call karo (National Cyber Crime Helpline)
2. **cybercrime.gov.in** pe shikayat
3. **Bank** ko turant batao — account/FD/transfer rukwao
4. Call/number **Sanchar Saathi (Chakshu)** pe report karo
5. Saboot sambhaalo — screenshot, number, transaction ID
6. Darr lage to **112**

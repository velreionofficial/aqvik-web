### Page 1 — Darwaza

Raat 1:20.

Neha ne darwaza khatkhataya. "Papa. Darwaza kholiye."

Andar se awaaz band ho gayi. Phir dhimi phusphusahat: *"…koi nahi sir, beti hai… haan sir, main sambhal leta hoon…"*

"Papa, maine reel suni hai." Neha ki awaaz kaanp rahi thi. "Aap 'sir' kisko bol rahe ho?"

Sannata.

Phir Bunty ne woh kiya jo sirf ek 17 saal ka ladka kar sakta hai. Usne darwaze pe kandha maara. Ek baar. Do baar. Purani kundi teesri baar mein jawaab de gayi.

Kamre mein Ramesh ji zameen pe baithe the. Phone almari pe khada tha. Screen pe ek vardi wala aadmi.

Neha ne phone ki taraf dekha. Vardi wale ne bhi Neha ko dekha.

"Ladki," usne thandi awaaz mein kaha, "aap ek **central investigation** mein dakhal de rahi hain. Abhi bahar jaiye, warna aap bhi —"

Neha ne phone uthaya. Aur pehli baar, paanch din mein, kisi ne **call kaat di.**

### Page 2 — Toota hua aadmi

Ramesh ji ne beti ki taraf dekha. Phir zameen ki taraf.

"Neha… mujhe jail jaana hoga. Maine sab de diya… sab… court ne kaha tha… refundable…"

Unki awaaz phat gayi. Ek retired engineer, jisne paintees saal mein ek rupaye ki gadbad nahi ki, apni beti ke saamne bachche ki tarah ro raha tha.

Sudha ji darwaze pe aa gayi thin. "Kya hua? Kitna… kitna diya?"

Ramesh ji ne kuch nahi kaha. Bas teen ungliyaan uthayin. Teen FD.

Sudha ji wahin baith gayin.

Bunty ne papa ka phone uthaya. Teen din ke chats. PDFs. Mohar. *"Surety_Order.pdf"*. *"RBI_Verification_Notice.pdf"*. Usne jaldi-jaldi screenshot lene shuru kiye. Haath kaanp rahe the, lekin wo ruka nahi.

Tabhi ghanti baji. Darwaze pe Arjun tha. Bike ki chaabi abhi bhi haath mein.

### Page 3 — Pehla ghanta

### Page 4 — 1930

"National Cyber Crime Helpline, main Priya bol rahi hoon. Aapki shikayat kya hai?"

Neha ne bolna shuru kiya. Arjun bagal mein baith ke tareekh, amount aur account ke naam dikhata raha — jo usne Ramesh ji ke bank app se nikaale the:

- 22 Oct — ₹48,00,000 — Shree Ganesh Traders, Jaipur
- 23 Oct — ₹62,00,000 — Maa Durga Enterprises, Kolkata
- 26 Oct — ₹28,00,000 — Sai Kripa Solutions, Hyderabad

"Ji madam. Aapki shikayat darj ho gayi hai. Ye details turant sambandhit banks ko bheji ja rahi hain taaki jo paisa abhi in accounts mein ya aage ke accounts mein bacha hai, use **hold** kiya ja sake. Aapka acknowledgement number SMS pe aayega. Kal **cybercrime.gov.in** pe ise poori detail ke saath complete kar dijiye, aur local cyber police station mein bhi jaaiye."

"Paisa wapas aayega?" Neha ne poochha.

Ek pal ki chuppi. "Madam, jitna paisa rok liya jaata hai, utna bachne ka mauka hota hai. Sabse aakhri transfer aaj ka hai — uske rukne ka mauka sabse zyada hai. Pehle wale… koshish ki jayegi."

Neha ne phone rakha. Arjun pehle hi apne bank ki fraud team ko call kar chuka tha. Ramesh ji ke account ke saare transactions pe *flag*.

Raat 2:15. Ghar mein koi nahi soya.

### Page 5 — Compound mein hadbadi

**Phnom Penh. Usi samay.**

Desk 117 pe laal light jali. *Target disconnected.*

Manager daudta aaya. "Kya hua?! Abhi to 'clearance fee' ka doosra round baaki tha!"

"Ghar walon ne call kaat di," Raju ne kaha. Uski awaaz mein kuch tha jo manager ko pasand nahi aaya.

"Number block karo. Agle target pe jao. Aur paisa — paisa abhi ke abhi aage ghumao. Saare accounts se!"

Hall mein teen ladke daude. Unka kaam tha paisa ek account se das accounts mein, das se sau accounts mein todna — **layers** — taaki police ke pahunchne tak paisa crypto ya nakad mein badal jaaye.

Ye daud thi. Ek taraf Lucknow ka ek parivaar aur 1930. Doosri taraf ek compound aur sau account.

Kuch accounts mein paisa pahunchne se pehle hi bank ne *hold* laga diya tha.

Kuch mein nahi.

### Page 6 — Inspector Farida Khan

**Agli subah, 10 baje. Cyber Crime Police Station, Lucknow.**

Inspector Farida Khan ne chai ka cup neeche rakha aur Bunty ke screenshots dekhe. Ek-ek karke.

"VidCall. Fake courtroom. 'RBI verification'. 'Refundable surety'." Unhone aah bhari. "Is mahine ka aathvaan case hai, Verma ji. Pichhle hafte ek retired doctor sahab the. Unse pehle ek bank manager."

"Bank manager bhi?" Ramesh ji ne dheere se poochha.

"Bank manager bhi. Ek judge ke rishtedaar bhi. Ye padhe-likhe, imaandaar logon ko chunte hain, Verma ji. Kyunki imaandaar aadmi ko *apne naam pe daag* se sabse zyada darr lagta hai. Aapki koi galti nahi thi."

Unhone FIR likhi. 1930 ka acknowledgement number joda. Bank se transaction trail maanga.

Phir ek cheez jo Arjun ne bataayi — bank cashier ne us din form ki photo li thi, aur CCTV mein Ramesh ji ki jeb mein jalta phone dikh raha tha. "Ye kaam aayega," Farida ji ne kaha. "Ye saabit karta hai ki aap dabaav mein the."

### Page 7 — Jaipur

**Teen hafte baad. Jaipur, ek PG ka kamra.**

"Karan Saini? Lucknow cyber police. Aapka account — *Shree Ganesh Traders* — current account?"

Karan ka chehra safed. "Wo… wo mera nahi hai sir. Matlab mera hai, lekin… ek bhaiya ne kaha tha ki unhe business ke liye ek current account chahiye, ₹5,000 mahina doonga, bas KYC tumhare naam se. Maine sirf documents diye the… OTP bata deta tha jab wo phone karte the…"

"Is account mein teen hafte pehle ek retired aadmi ki zindagi bhar ki kamai ke ₹48 lakh aaye, aur do ghante mein 14 accounts mein chale gaye." Farida ji ki awaaz sakht nahi thi. Thaki hui thi. "Aapko ₹5,000 mile. Unhe kya mila?"

### Page 12 — Case 1 ka saar

**🚩 Digital Arrest ko kaise pehchaanein — 5 nishaan**
1. Courier/parcel, Aadhaar ya "aapke naam pe FIR" wali **anjaan call**
2. Call ko "police/CBI/court" pe **transfer** karna
3. **Video call pe "arrest"**, camera on rakhne ka hukm
4. **"Kisi ko mat batana"** — ghar walon se kaatna
5. **Paisa "verify/surety/tax"** ke naam pe kisi account mein bhejne ko kehna

**✅ Ruko, Socho, Action lo**
- **Ruko:** ghabrao mat. Koi bhi detail mat do. Call kaato.
- **Socho:** koi sarkari agency video call pe dhamki nahi deti, na paisa maangti hai. "Digital arrest" kanoon mein hai hi nahi.
- **Action lo:** ghar walon ko batao, **1930** lagao, police ko batao.

**🆘 Agar aapke saath ho jaye**
1. **1930** — turant, din ho ya raat (pehle kuch ghante sabse keemti)
2. **cybercrime.gov.in** — shikayat poori karo, acknowledgement number sambhaalo
3. **Bank** ko batao — account aur transfers pe hold
4. Number **Sanchar Saathi (Chakshu)** pe report karo
5. **Saboot** — screenshots, PDFs, SMS, account naam, kuch delete mat karo
6. Darr ho to **112**, aur najdeeki **cyber police station** jaao

### Page 1 — Sangeeta

Ravi raat 11 baje ghar pahuncha. Pari so chuki thi — uski shirt pakad ke, jo Sangeeta ne takiye pe rakh di thi.

Sangeeta ne kuch nahi poochha. Pehle khaana garam kiya. Phir saamne baithi.

"Ab batao. Shuru se."

Ravi ne bataya. ₹6,000. Pari ki fees. PaisaTurant. Nau app. ₹1,38,000. Neend wali raatein. Photo.

Sangeeta sunti rahi. Ek baar bhi beech mein nahi boli. Jab Ravi chup hua, usne ek hi sawaal poocha:

"Chhe hafte. Mujhe ek baar bhi kyun nahi bataya?"

"Mard hoon, Sangeeta. Socha main—"

"Mard ho, isliye akele dubna tha?" Sangeeta ki awaaz kaanpi, phir sambhali. "Shaadi mein saath phere liye the, Ravi. Saath daldal mein bhi utarenge. Aur saath nikalenge."

Phir usne Ravi ka phone uthaya. Nau app ki list banayi. Aur ek faisla sunaaya:

"Bas. Aaj se ek paisa nahi. Ek bhi nahi."

### Page 3 — 1930 aur Sachet

:::dialer
number: 1930
label: National Cyber Crime Helpline
:::

Subah Sangeeta ne 1930 pe call kiya, aur phir Ravi ke saath cybercrime.gov.in pe poori shikayat daali — nau app ke naam, dhamki wale numbers ke screenshot, photo, har payment ka UTR.

Phir Ravi ke college ke ek dost ne, jo bank mein tha, ek aur cheez batayi.

"RBI ki website pe **Digital Lending Apps ki ek directory** hai. Jo app RBI-regulated bank ya NBFC se jude hain, unka naam wahan hota hai."

Ravi ne PaisaTurant dhoondha. Nahi mila. QuickRupee — nahi. Nau mein se ek bhi nahi.

"Aur RBI ka **Sachet** portal hai," dost ne kaha. "Wahan in unregistered lenders ki shikayat karo. Taaki inhe band karne ki list mein naam jaaye."

Sangeeta ne wo bhi kiya. Phir sabhi nau app ki permission hatai — Contacts, Photos, Location — aur unhe phone se mita diya.

Us din phone 23 baar baja. Alag-alag numbers. Ravi ne ek bhi nahi uthaya. Har number ko **Chakshu** pe report kiya.

### Page 6 — Chaapa

**Teen hafte baad.**

Teesri manzil wale us hall pe chaapa pada. **42 caller, 3 trainer, 2 manager** pakde gaye. 70 computer, 200 se zyada SIM, aur ek server jisme **1.2 lakh logon** ke contacts aur photos the. Unki marzi ke bina.

Asli maalik — jo sirf video call pe dikhta tha, kisi doosre desh se — haath nahi aaya. Police ke mutabik paisa kai "kiraye ke khaaton" se hote hue crypto mein jaata tha.

Ranchi cyber police ne Ravi ko bulaaya.

"Ravi ji, aapke paise jin khaaton mein gaye, unme se do mein **₹18,600 freeze** hue hain. Court ke order se wapas aayenge. Baaki aage nikal gaya."

### Page 10 — Case 8 ka saar

**🚩 Nakli loan app ko kaise pehchaanein — 6 nishaan**
1. **"No credit score, no documents, 5 minute"** — bina jaanch ka loan
2. **Contacts, Photos, Location ki permission** maange
3. **Haath mein kam, wapas bahut zyada** — chhupi fees, 7 din ka loan
4. **RBI ki Digital Lending Apps directory** mein naam nahi
5. **Due date se pehle dhamki**, bharne ke baad "payment nahi dikha"
6. **Ek app se doosra app** — "pre-approved offer"

**✅ Agar loan chahiye**
- Pehle **bank, apni company ka salary advance, ya ghar** — app aakhri raasta
- App ka naam **RBI ki directory** mein check karo
- **Key Fact Statement** maango — asli rakam, fees, byaaj sab likha hota hai
- Har mahine thoda sa **"mushkil waqt" ka gullak** — chhota sahi, pakka

**🆘 Agar aapke saath ho jaye**
1. **1930** aur **cybercrime.gov.in** — dhamki, photo, call ke screenshot ke saath
2. **Apne contacts ko pehle se bata do** — "mere naam se nakli photo aaye to ignore karein"
3. **RBI Sachet portal** (sachet.rbi.org.in) pe app ki shikayat
4. Permission hatao, app mitao, dhamki wale number **Chakshu** pe report karo
5. **Akele mat jhelo.** Ghar mein batao. Man bahut bhaari ho to **Tele-MANAS 14416** — muft, 24 ghante, aapki bhasha mein.

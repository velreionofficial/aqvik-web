**Ranchi. Lalpur ka ek delivery hub. Budhvaar, dopahar 2:10.**

Ravi ki bike pe 14 parcel bandhe the. Phone baja. Hub manager **Faruqui sir**.

"Ravi. Abhi ke abhi hub aao."

Hub pe Faruqui sir ne apna phone uski taraf ghumaya. Screen pe Ravi ki photo thi — uski ID card wali. Upar laal akshar mein likha tha:

*"CHOR! ⚠ Ye aadmi RAVI KUMAR MAHTO ₹6,000 loan lekar bhaag gaya hai. Iska saath dene wala bhi doshi. Ise jaante ho to paise dilwao."*

"Ye mere phone pe aaya hai," Faruqui sir ne kaha. "Aur hamare 40 rider ke group mein bhi. Aur ek aur photo aayi hai, Ravi…" unhone nazrein jhuka leen, "…jo main tumhe dikha bhi nahi sakta."

Ravi ke kaanon mein seeti bajne lagi.

₹6,000. Usne sirf ₹6,000 liye the. Aur pichhle chhe hafte mein, nau alag-alag apps ko, wo **₹1,38,000** de chuka tha.

Aur karz ab bhi khatam nahi hua tha.

## Is part ki seekh

**🚩 Is part mein thag ki chaalein**
1. **"No credit score, no documents, 5 minute":** asli bank ya RBI-regulated lender aise loan nahi baant-te.
2. **Contacts, Photos, Location ki "Allow":** loan ke liye inki koi zarurat nahi. Ye dhamki ka saamaan hai.
3. **Haath mein kam, wapas zyada:** ₹6,000 ka loan, ₹3,780 haath mein, 7 din mein ₹6,000.
4. **Due date se pehle dhamki, bharne ke baad "late fee":** taaki aap kabhi baahar na nikal pao.
5. **Ek app se doosra app:** "pre-approved offer" — karz chukaane ke liye naya karz. Daldal.
6. **Nakli/ashleel photo aur "CHOR" poster:** sharm se chup karaana. Yahi unka asli hathiyaar hai.

**✅ Bachaav**
- Loan sirf **RBI-regulated bank/NBFC** se. RBI ki website pe **Digital Lending Apps ki directory** mein app ka naam check karo.
- Koi loan app **Contacts ya Photos** maange — wahin band karo, uninstall karo.
- Loan lene se pehle **asli rakam, fees aur byaaj (APR)** likhit mein dekho. Asli lender ek **Key Fact Statement** deta hai.
- Mushkil ho to **ghar walon ko batao.** Chuppi thag ki sabse badi taakat hai.

**🆘 Agar aapke saath ho jaye**
1. **1930** pe call aur **cybercrime.gov.in** pe shikayat — dhamki ke call, message, photo ke screenshot ke saath
2. Apne contacts ko **pehle se** message bhejo: "Mere naam se koi nakli photo/message aaye to ignore karein, police mein shikayat hai."
3. **RBI ke Sachet portal** (sachet.rbi.org.in) pe us app ki shikayat
4. App ki permission hatao, uninstall karo; number **Sanchar Saathi (Chakshu)** pe report karo
5. Man bahut bhaari ho to **Tele-MANAS 14416** pe baat karo — muft, 24 ghante. Aap akele nahi ho.

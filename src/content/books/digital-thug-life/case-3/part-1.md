## Is part ki seekh

**🚩 Is part mein thag ki chaalein**
1. **Darr + ghadi:** "Account aaj BLOCK hoga", ulti chalti ghadi — taaki aap link ka naam dhyan se na dekho.
2. **Nakli website:** bank jaisa logo aur rang, lekin link ka naam (domain) bank ka nahi. Thag har din naye naam bana lete hain.
3. **.apk file:** "KYC app", "e-challan", "shaadi ka card", "bijli bill" — naam kuch bhi, asal mein ek app jo aapke SMS padhta hai aur aapke naam se aage phailta hai.
4. **Gayab icon, chhupe SMS:** app apna icon chhupa leta hai aur OTP wale SMS aapke dekhne se pehle aage bhej deta hai. Isliye raat ko sab "normal" lagta hai.

**✅ Bachaav**
- **Bank kabhi SMS ya chat ke link se KYC nahi karwata.** KYC ke liye branch jao, ya bank ki official app/website khud kholo.
- **Kabhi bhi link se aayi .apk file install mat karo** — chahe bhejne wala apna hi kyon na ho. Apps sirf Play Store/App Store se.
- Phone poochhe *"Is file se nuksaan ho sakta hai"* — to **ruk jao.** Wo warning isi din ke liye hai.
- Koi app **SMS padhne ki ijaazat** maange — mana karo. Bank app ko ye nahi chahiye.

**🆘 Agar aapke saath ho jaye**
1. **Bank ko turant call** (card/passbook pe likha number) — saare khaate, net banking, UPI block
2. **1930** pe call, aur **cybercrime.gov.in** pe shikayat
3. Phone ka **internet band** karo; kisi doosre phone se net banking/email ke password badlo
4. Anjaan app hatao — na hate to phone **factory reset** ke liye kisi bharosemand jaankaar ki madad lo
5. Apne **contacts ko turant batao** ki aapke naam se aayi file na kholein
6. Link/number **Sanchar Saathi (Chakshu)** pe report karo

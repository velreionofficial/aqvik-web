### Page 11 — Case 3 ka saar

**🚩 Nakli KYC link aur APK ko kaise pehchaanein — 5 nishaan**
1. **"Account aaj block ho jayega"** — darr aur ulti chalti ghadi
2. **SMS/chat mein link** — aur link ka naam bank ki asli website jaisa *nahi*
3. **.apk file** — "KYC app", "e-challan", "shaadi ka card", "bijli bill", "photo"
4. Phone ki chetavani: **"Is file se nuksaan ho sakta hai"**
5. App ka **SMS padhne ki ijaazat** maangna

**✅ Bachaav**
- KYC: **branch mein, ya bank ki official app khud khol ke.** Link se kabhi nahi.
- Apps **sirf Play Store/App Store** se. Link se aayi .apk — kabhi nahi, chahe apna hi bheje.
- Mahine mein ek baar *Settings → Apps* dekho — **anjaan naam, bina icon** wala app ho to ghar ke kisi jaankaar ko dikhao.
- Ghar ke bade logon ke phone mein **Play Store ke bahar se install** ka option band karwa do.

**🆘 Agar aapke saath ho jaye**
1. **Bank** ko turant (passbook/card pe likha number) — saare khaate, net banking, UPI, card block
2. Phone ka **internet band** (airplane mode)
3. **1930** aur **cybercrime.gov.in**
4. **Saboot pehle, reset baad mein** — app ka naam, permissions, messages ke screenshot
5. Kisi doosre phone se **passwords badlo**
6. **Contacts ko batao** — "mere naam se aayi file mat kholna"
7. Link/number **Sanchar Saathi (Chakshu)** pe report

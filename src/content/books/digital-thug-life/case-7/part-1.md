## Is part ki seekh

**🚩 Is part mein thag ki chaalein**
1. **"Free masterclass" aur "Professor":** bade naam, nakli anubhav, awaaz badalne wala app.
2. **Pehle sach mein munaafa:** chaar group, chaar tip — jo chal gayi, wahi dikhai.
3. **Nakli app:** app store ka nahi, seedha link se. Usme profit sirf ek number hai jo thag type karta hai.
4. **"Institutional account", "pakka IPO allotment":** aisa kuch hota hi nahi.
5. **Company ka paisa kisi "Traders" ke khaate mein:** asli broker ko paisa kabhi kisi tisre naam ke khaate mein nahi jaata.
6. **Chhoti nikaasi chalne dena:** ₹10,000 wapas — taaki ₹47 lakh aayein.

**✅ Bachaav**
- Koi bhi advisor ya broker — **SEBI ki website pe registration khud check karo.** "SEBI Check" se UPI handle bhi check hota hai.
- Asli registered broker/advisor ka UPI handle **"@valid"** pe khatam hota hai. Kisi "Shree Ganesh Traders" ko paisa = laal jhanda.
- Aapke shares **aapke apne demat** mein hote hain. CDSL/NSDL ka statement (CAS) dekho — jo wahan nahi, wo aapka nahi.
- IPO ki arzi sirf **apne bank (ASBA) ya broker ke app** se. "Pakka allotment" = jhooth.
- **Ghar mein batao.** "Surprise" ke chakkar mein chupaaya gaya paisa thag ke liye sabse aasaan shikaar hai.

**🆘 Agar aapke saath ho jaye**
1. **1930** pe turant call — har transfer ki tareekh, rakam aur UTR ke saath
2. **cybercrime.gov.in** pe shikayat — group, app, chat aur payment ke screenshot
3. **Bank** ko — jin khaaton mein paisa gaya, unki jaankaari
4. **SEBI SCORES** pe bhi shikayat kar sakte ho
5. "Withdraw ke liye fee" — ek rupaya aur mat bhejo

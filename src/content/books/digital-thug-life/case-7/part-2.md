### Page 10 — Case 7 ka saar

**🚩 Nakli trading app / "sure-shot tip" scam ko kaise pehchaanein — 6 nishaan**
1. **Ad se "free masterclass"**, phir ChatBox group, roz ke "sure-shot" tips
2. **Bada naam, bada daava** — "Professor", "ex-fund manager", "27 saal anubhav"
3. **App store se nahi, link se app** — "institutional platform"
4. **Paisa kisi "Traders" ya vyakti ke khaate mein** — broker ke naam pe nahi
5. **"Pakka IPO allotment", "block deal 30% discount"**
6. **Nikaalne ke liye pehle fee/tax** — aur phir "recovery officer" ka call

**✅ Paanch niyam**
- **SEBI ki website pe registration check** — advisor, broker, sab
- Paisa sirf **apne demat/broker** ko — registered ka UPI **"@valid"** pe khatam hota hai, "SEBI Check" se jaancho
- **CDSL/NSDL statement (CAS)** — jo wahan nahi, wo aapka nahi
- IPO sirf **ASBA (apne bank) ya broker ke app** se — "pakka allotment" jaisa kuch nahi hota
- Bada faisla **parivaar ke saath** — surprise ke chakkar mein nahi

**🆘 Agar aapke saath ho jaye**
1. **1930** — turant, saare UTR ke saath
2. **cybercrime.gov.in** — group, app ka naam, chat, payment ke screenshot
3. **SEBI SCORES** pe shikayat — taaki agla shikaar bache
4. **Bank** ko batao
5. **"Recovery fee" wale kisi call pe ek rupaya nahi** — SEBI, police ya court phone pe fee nahi maangte

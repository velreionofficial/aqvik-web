### Page 10 — Case 4 ka saar

**🚩 UPI / QR / "fauji" scam ko kaise pehchaanein — 5 nishaan**
1. Khareedaar **bina dekhe, bina mol-bhaav** poora paisa dene ko taiyaar
2. **"Fauji hoon, posting/transfer, cash nahi chalta"**
3. Paise "bhejne" ke liye **QR, collect request ya PIN** maangna
4. **"Galti se kata, double wapas karta hoon"**
5. Bechne wala **bahut sasta** saamaan + **advance** maange ("army courier")

**✅ Ek line yaad rakho: PIN = paise dena**
- Paise lene ke liye **kabhi PIN, QR scan ya OTP nahi lagta.**
- Saamaan bech rahe ho: **aapka QR, unka scan.** Kabhi ulta nahi.
- Paisa aaya ya nahi — **apna bank app khud kholke dekho**, kisi screenshot pe bharosa nahi.
- Khareed rahe ho: **saamaan dekhe bina advance nahi.**

**🆘 Agar aapke saath ho jaye**
1. **1930** — turant
2. **Bank** — UPI block, shikayat
3. **cybercrime.gov.in** — UPI ID, QR ki photo, chat ke screenshot ke saath
4. Number **Sanchar Saathi (Chakshu)** pe, aur account ko us app pe **report**
5. "Paisa wapas dilaane" wale kisi call pe bharosa nahi — wo doosra thag hai

**💬 Ise share karo:** un sabke saath jo purana saamaan online bechte-khareedte hain. Aapka QR, unka scan.

## Is part ki seekh

**🚩 Is part mein thag ki chaalein**
1. **Fauj ke naam ka galat istemaal:** vardi wali photo aur "ID card" — internet se uthaaye. Thag fauj ki izzat ko apna hathiyaar banate hain.
2. **Bargaining nahi, jaldi haan:** asli khareedaar mol-bhaav karta hai. Bina mol-bhaav "poora advance" = laal jhanda.
3. **"Paise bhejne ke liye QR/PIN":** collect request ya QR pe likha ho "Receive" — asal mein wo **pay** hai.
4. **"Galti se kata, double wapas karunga":** jitna gaya, use wapas laane ke liye aap aur daalte jaate ho. Kabhi wapas nahi aata.

**✅ Bachaav**
- **Paise LENE ke liye kabhi PIN, QR scan ya OTP nahi lagta.** PIN = paise dena. Hamesha.
- Collect request pe **"Pay"** likha ho to wo aapke khaate se paisa kaatega — note mein kuch bhi likha ho.
- Purana saamaan bech rahe ho? **Khareedaar ko ghar aake dekhne do, cash ya seedha transfer lo** — aur transfer aane ke baad bank app mein khud check karo.
- "Fauji", "transfer", "posting", "cash nahi chalta" — ye sab ek hi script ke dialogue hain. Asli faujiyon ki izzat karo; nakli ki pehchaan rakho.

**🆘 Agar aapke saath ho jaye**
1. **1930** pe turant call
2. **Bank** ko call — UPI block, transactions ki shikayat
3. **cybercrime.gov.in** pe shikayat — UPI ID, QR ki photo, chat ke screenshot ke saath
4. Number **Sanchar Saathi (Chakshu)** pe report karo
5. Us app (jaise BechDo) pe us account ko **report** karo
6. Aage "wapas dilaane" wale kisi bhi call/message pe bharosa mat karo — wo doosra thag hota hai

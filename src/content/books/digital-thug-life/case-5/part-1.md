Kamla Devi ne card nikaal ke dekha. Avadh National Bank. Neela rang. Wahi chip. Lekin card pe chhapa naam — *SHABNAM BANO.*

Unka naam nahi tha.

Kamla Devi wahin khadi reh gayin. Haath mein ek anjaan aurat ka card. Aur unka apna card — jisme unki 32 saal ki naukri ki pension aati thi — kahin aur. Kisi aur ke haath mein.

Unhe abhi pata nahi tha ki pichhle 22 ghante mein us card se **₹1,42,500** nikal chuke the.

## Is part ki seekh

**🚩 Is part mein thag ki chaalein**
1. **"Madad" ka bahana:** ATM pe koi anjaan "main dikha deta hoon" kahe — wahi sabse bada laal jhanda hai.
2. **PIN dekhna:** muh pher ke bhi PIN dekha ja sakta hai — screen ki parchhai, kaanch, ya kandhe ke upar se.
3. **Card ki adla-badli:** wahi bank, wahi rang ka doosra card wapas haath mein — farq sirf naam ka.
4. **Bujurg aur SMS na padhne wale:** thag jaante hain ki unke paas ghanton ka samay hai.

**✅ Bachaav**
- ATM pe **kisi anjaan ki madad mat lo.** Machine atke to bank ke guard ya branch se poochho — ya ghar wale ke saath aao.
- PIN daalte waqt **doosre haath se keypad dhako.** Kisi ko kiosk ke andar mat aane do.
- ATM se nikalte hi **card pe apna naam padh lo.** Ek second ka kaam.
- Phone pe **bank ke SMS / app alert chalu rakho**, aur ghar ke kisi bachche ka number bhi jodo jo padhta ho.
- Bank app mein **ATM / card ki daily limit kam karo**, aur jab zarurat na ho to card **"off"** kar do.

**🆘 Agar aapke saath ho jaye**
1. **Bank** ko turant call — card **block**. Number bank ki official app ya website se lo.
2. **1930** pe call — har transaction ka samay aur jagah batao
3. **cybercrime.gov.in** pe shikayat — SMS ke screenshot, galat card ki photo ke saath
4. **Galat card sambhaal ke rakho** — police ke liye saboot hai
5. **Najdeeki thane** mein bhi shikayat — ATM aur dukaan ke CCTV jaldi maangne padte hain
6. "Paisa wapas dilaane" wale kisi call pe bharosa mat karo — wo doosra thag hai

### Page 2 — Bank

**Dopahar 12 baje. Avadh National Bank, Mansarovar branch.**

Branch manager **Saxena ji** ne shikayat likhi. Sab transaction dekhe. Phir chashma utaar ke kaha:

"Mataji, main aapse seedhi baat karunga. Aapki shikayat hum aage bhejenge, jaanch hogi. Lekin ek baat samajh lijiye — ye transaction **aapke asli card aur sahi PIN** se hue hain. Aapne ek anjaan aadmi ko card diya, aur uske saamne PIN daala."

"Lekin wo madad—"

"Jaanta hoon, mataji. Lekin RBI ke niyam mein, jab grahak khud card ya PIN kisi ko de deta hai — chahe bharose mein, chahe dhokhe se — to report karne tak ka nuksaan aksar grahak pe hi aata hai. Bank ki galti nahi maani jaati."

Kamla ji chup rahin. Kabir ka gussa phoot pada. "To bank kuch nahi karega?"

"Karenge, beta. Card block hai. Jaanch hogi. Police ko saare record denge — ATM ka CCTV bhi. Lekin paisa wapas aayega ya nahi, ye police pe zyada nirbhar hai."

Saxena ji ne ek pal ruk ke kaha, "Aur haan — agle hafte koi 'bank se' phone kare aur paisa wapas dilaane ke liye OTP ya PIN maange, to samajh lena wo doosra thag hai. Hum kabhi nahi maangte."

### Page 10 — Case 5 ka saar

**🚩 ATM fraud ko kaise pehchaanein — 5 nishaan**
1. Koi anjaan kahe **"main dikha deta hoon"** — madad ke bahaane
2. Koi **kiosk ke andar** aaye, ya kandhe ke upar se dekhe
3. Machine **atke**, aur koi turant "doosre ATM" ki salaah de
4. Card slot **dheela, mota, ya alag rang ka** lage; keypad ke upar kuch chipka ho
5. Card aapki jeb mein, aur **SMS kahe paisa nikla** — card ki copy bani hai

**✅ Teen line yaad rakho**
- **Anjaan ki madad nahi.** Guard, branch, ya ghar wala.
- **PIN daalte waqt haath se dhako.** Har baar.
- **Card nikalte hi naam padho.** Ek second.
- Bank app mein **daily limit kam** rakho, aur card ko zarurat na ho to **"off"**.
- Bank ke **SMS alert** padho — ya ghar ke kisi bachche ka number bhi jodo.

**🆘 Agar aapke saath ho jaye**
1. **Bank** — card turant block (number official app/website se)
2. **1930** — turant
3. **cybercrime.gov.in** — SMS ke screenshot aur galat card ki photo ke saath
4. **Thane** mein shikayat — ATM aur dukaan ka CCTV jaldi maangna padta hai
5. **Card aapke paas tha aur PIN kisi ko nahi diya?** RBI ke niyam se 3 working din ke andar report karo — zimmedaari zero. Der na karo.
6. Fake caller ka number **Sanchar Saathi (Chakshu)** pe report karo
